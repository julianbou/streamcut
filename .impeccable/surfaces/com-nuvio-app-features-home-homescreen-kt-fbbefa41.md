# Home (clipper build)

Scope: the desktop home tab when `AppFeaturePolicy.viewingChromeEnabled` is false. Mode: Operate.
Audience/job: a clip-maker who already knows the film they want. Get them to it (search) or back to one they opened (recents) in one action; browsing addon catalogs is the last resort.
Confirmed: recents = titles opened in the player only (no next-up, no release alerts); search results render inline on home, the sidebar Search tab is hidden in the clipper build; "Your clips" leave home (Library keeps them); catalogs sit collapsed at the bottom and load only when opened.
Pinned by user: grainy gradients, organic feel; this world will later spread to the whole app.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Home is the user's own repertory programme, riso-printed: a search masthead and the films they have been cutting, not a streaming storefront of rows and heroes.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black plum stock (#141019). Translucent riso inks that overprint: fluorescent pink #FF48B0 (act/search), riso blue #3D5AFE (where you were), sunflower #FFD84A (set aside, same meaning as the player's amber ranges). Inks appear as soft organic blooms with stochastic grain and slight misregistration; soft bloom edges divide sections, never boxes, rules or cards-in-cards. Paper-white text #F2ECE4. Posters keep their own colour.

STORY: Open the app, type the film, it appears as you type; or pick up a film you were cutting where you left it. Catalogs are there if you truly need to browse.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top two-thirds: a pink-into-blue grain bloom behind a masthead-scale search field (the field's text is the largest type in the app), keyboard hint beside it. Below: "Recently opened" as programme listings (poster, title, where you stopped as a blue position mark). Nothing else above the fold. Typing swaps the listings for results in place.

FORM: Riso Repertory Programme, position 5 on the grounded list, seed key 5f6f504a. Raises: bloom edges divide (zoo map), one meaning per ink (gravity garden), the listing is the action (HyperCard), results as you type (vertical feed). Signature motion: blooms drift slowly; focusing search swells the pink bloom.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
